Where it started

Shahara did not begin with a studio or a shop. She began with a love of making things and the faith to keep going.

  1. A creative spark

    Art has always been how she says what words can't. Drawing and painting came first, and the rest grew from there.

  2. Making for people she loves

    She paints chains and gives them away as gifts. When someone asked what a painted Jet Life chain would cost, she said it was meant for the artist. The giving came before the price tag.

  3. Back to the hats

    She shares her work with thousands of followers, and her latest announcement was simple: "Back making hats, message me if interested."

  4. Now, a home of her own

    This site gives every piece a place that belongs to her, outside of social media.
